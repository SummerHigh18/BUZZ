# March 27th: Session 1 - Schematic

I started off by designing the schematic, in which I added the XIAO RP2040 and 3 LEDs, resistors and buttons.
Then for some personal touch, I added a rotary switch and a buzzer.
(Took some time for understanding the rotary pins and schematic)
At last I assigned footprints to respective components.

# March 27th: Session 2 - PCB
Now comes the PCB part, which took a lot of time(& efforts...)
I designed my basic PCB layout and design in Paint, and then implemented it in KiCAD.
After placing the components, and defining the Edge.Cut layer, I completed the wiring stuff.
Then, I added all the 3D parts to each one of them.
